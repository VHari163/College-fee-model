Management Modules

Use the modules below to manage different parts of the college fee management system.

👨‍🎓 Students

View and manage student information, hostel status and transport status.

🏢 Departments

Add and manage college departments such as CSE, CSD, ECE, EEE and others.

📚 Courses

Manage B.Tech branches and connect them with their departments.

📅 Academic Years

Manage active and previous academic years.

💰 Fee Types

Add and manage tuition, exam, library, hostel, bus and other fees.

📋 Fee Structures

Configure course-wise fees with academic year, amount and due date.

💳 Payments

View and manage student payment transactions.
